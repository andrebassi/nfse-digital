[`scripts/coverage-gate.sh`](https://github.com/andrebassi/nfse-digital/blob/main/scripts/coverage-gate.sh) mede cobertura **por pacote** e **reprova** (rc=1) abaixo do piso — `go test -cover` sozinho só imprime e sai 0. Pisos: `internal/domain` **100%**, demais pacotes de `internal/` **90%**. Isentos: `cmd/` (wiring) e `internal/ports` (só interfaces). Prova de falha: `COVERAGE_FLOOR=101 task test:cov` tem que reprovar.
